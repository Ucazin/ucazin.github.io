Estudio Canais Lucca

A personal desktop tool for publishing my own animated short videos on my own YouTube channels.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

What it is

I create original animated short videos (YouTube Shorts) for a small network of YouTube channels that I own and manage from a single Google account. Each series has a Portuguese channel and an English channel. Estudio Canais Lucca is a script that runs only on my own computer and publishes those videos for me through the YouTube Data API v3, instead of uploading each one by hand in YouTube Studio.

What it does

  • Lists the channels managed by my Google account, so each video goes to the right channel.
  • Uploads each finished video with its title, description, tags, language and category.
  • Schedules the publish date and time of each video.
  • Adds caption files for the videos and adds each episode to its series playlist.

It does not read, collect or display data from any other YouTube user. It has no website login, no accounts and no server.

Who uses it

Only me, the developer, on my own channels. The tool is not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else.

Google permissions and why they are needed

ScopeUsed for
youtube.uploadUploading my videos and setting their scheduled publish time.
youtube.force-sslAdding captions, updating my videos' details and adding them to my playlists.
